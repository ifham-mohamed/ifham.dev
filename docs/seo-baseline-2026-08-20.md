## Baseline interpretation

- The homepage accounts for every recorded click and nearly all impressions.
- Twenty of 21 clicks came from Sri Lanka; the single remaining click came from Australia. Global impressions exist, but no other country produced a click in this window.
- Mobile and desktop impressions are nearly even in the three-month view, but mobile CTR is 7.1% versus 18.1% on desktop.
- Visible non-brand query evidence is limited to the TypeScript/clean-code cluster, with no clicks and positions 38.5–49.0.
- `/blog/typescript-best-practices` is the strongest visible non-brand content signal by impressions (9) and sits just outside the position-4–20 opportunity band at 21.8.
- `/blog/building-design-systems` and `/projects/total-supply` have positions inside the opportunity band, but only three and two impressions respectively. They need more post-release data before a ranking-driven rewrite is justified.
